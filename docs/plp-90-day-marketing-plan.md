# 90-day marketing preparation

Internal pre-launch checklist only. The calendar starts at implementation kickoff, but public release depends on licensing clearance and owner approval, not the dates. Public campaigns, paid advertising, OTA distribution, outbound social posts and commission earning stay off until those conditions are met. Completing this checklist does not open the public site or activate payments.

Track progress on Administration → Growth & Marketing. This file is the canonical outline.

## Days 1–30 — Internal systems operational

- Keep the website private behind the preview gate.
- Finish independent PLP branding and the official sales-contact change (Argie Concepcion).
- Inventory authorized photos and video of the Boracay property.
- Keep the invite-only affiliate architecture in place; commissions stay inactive.
- Confirm the centralized inquiry workflow and Sales Inbox path for Argie.

Exit: internal systems are operational. Still private.

## Days 31–60 — Marketing assets and workflows approved

- Produce the one-minute promo (see `docs/plp-promo-video-brief.md`) and vertical social edits from authorized footage only.
- Prepare Facebook, Instagram and TikTok content in private drafts. Do not publish, boost or run ads.
- Build a content calendar for use after launch clearance.
- Referral testing is limited to internal staff and owners. Do not recruit or onboard external affiliates while unlicensed.

Exit: marketing assets and workflows are approved. Still private. No public posts.

## Days 61–90 — Launch-ready, still private

- Rehearse inquiry handling and (when authorized) booking walkthroughs on private/test paths only. No live public payment tests.
- Verify tracking and official social profile readiness without publishing campaigns.
- Train owners and staff on the inbox, preview gate and launch conditions.

Exit: launch-ready, but still private until cleared.

## Launch rule

The public site becomes eligible to open only when all four are true:

1. Required licenses and operating permissions are verified.
2. Doctora authorizes launch.
3. Official sales contact, business domain, public content and payment readiness are verified.
4. Security, bookings, inquiry handling and production acceptance tests pass.

Until then, keep public operations, commissions and campaigns disabled. There is no automatic public activation.

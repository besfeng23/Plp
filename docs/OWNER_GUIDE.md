# PLP Private Office — Owner guide

For the owners of Pueblo La Perla Boracay. No technical knowledge is needed.

## Signing in
1. Open **https://plp-boracay.vercel.app/owner** and choose **Open secure sign-in**.
2. Enter your owner email address and press **Send my private sign-in link**.
3. Open the email on the same device and tap the link — or type the 6–8 digit code into the sign-in page.
4. Sessions end automatically after 6 hours. Choose **Sign out** on shared devices.

Only approved owner email addresses can sign in. Staff access codes cannot open the Private Office.

## What each screen does
- **Overview** — this month at a glance: reservations, deposits, Pearl Club and open decisions.
- **Website Analytics** — visits and booking interest from consenting website visitors.
- **Resort Performance** — monthly reservations and deposits (amounts are in **USD**).
- **Pearl Club** — membership applications and activities for Boracay.
- **My Decisions** — your decision register. Add an item, approve or decline it, assign it to the team with a due date.
- **Expansion** — notes on future chapters (Singapore, Malaysia). Nothing here is public.
- **Reports & Documents** — download the monthly report and keep private documents (PDF, images, office files up to 8 MB).
- **Private Office Settings** — how your access and privacy are protected.

## Understanding payments
- Every room is **USD 1,000 per night**. Guests pay a **30% deposit** online through PayPal.
- A deposit shows as *verified* only after PayPal confirms the exact amount and currency.
- A stay is confirmed by the resort team after the deposit is verified; paying a deposit alone does not confirm it.

## Getting help
Ask the PLP operations contact listed in OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md. Never send passwords or codes by chat or email.

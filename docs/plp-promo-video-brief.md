# One-minute PLP promotional video brief

Shot list for a 60-second master of Pueblo La Perla Boracay. Internal only until launch is cleared. Use authorized, genuine footage of the property. Do not use stock, reference-site or placeholder imagery in any cut, asset library or deck. Use licensed music only. Do not invent amenities, fabricate guest testimonials or present other locations as operating PLP properties.

Internal previews carry a private-preview designation. Do not add a public booking call to action.

## Shot list

| Time | Beat | Brief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00–10 sec | The setting | Real authorized aerial footage of the hillside, ocean and villas. Introduce Pueblo La Perla Boracay. |
| 10–25 sec | The accommodations | Actual footage of Grand Ocean Villa, Sunset Suite and Smart Room Premium. |
| 25–40 sec | The experience | Genuine pool, terrace and privacy-focused property scenes. |
| 40–53 sec | Boracay atmosphere | Real island footage, sunset and approved guest-experience scenes. |
| 53–60 sec | Brand finish | PLP logo, understated tagline, and the approved official domain or inquiry channel. Until `pueblolaperla.com` is purchased and approved, use the official inquiry channel (Argie’s sales number / WhatsApp), not a public booking CTA. |

## Deliverables

- One 60-second landscape master.
- Vertical social edits, captions and poster frames.
- Approved cuts prepared for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, held unpublished until launch is cleared.

Spain, Malaysia and Indonesia are not filmed or captioned as operating PLP locations.

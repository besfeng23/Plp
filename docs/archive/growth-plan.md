## Pending owner confirmations

These four commercial inputs were the only immediate items requested from
the owner or property manager. They are the items kept on the Growth &
Marketing screen.

| Item | What is needed |
|---|---|
| Rates + capacity | Approved prices, inclusions and maximum occupancy for each bookable unit. |
| Available dates | Current December–February inventory and who can confirm reservations. |
| Arrival + transport | Actual address/access, beach route and available evening transport. |
| Official payments | Approved deposit method and the authorized recipient or payment link. |

Never invent rates, availability, transport or payment details.

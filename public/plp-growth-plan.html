01

The plan in one minute

Pueblo La Perla already has a website and an online presence. Now we want them to do more than look attractive. We want the right travelers to find PLP, understand the stay, contact the concierge and confidently book.

Our business goal

Generate more qualified accommodation enquiries and more confirmed stays—not merely more likes or followers.

1 weekTo prepare the essential enquiry experience and first proof videos, subject to verified details and footage.
3 videosReal walkthroughs that answer the questions guests ask before booking.
30 daysTo establish a repeatable marketing and follow-up process, then evaluate real results.
02

How a guest becomes a booking

The work has one straightforward purpose: guide prospective guests from seeing the property to speaking with the right person.

01

See PLP

Discover property videos and photos on Facebook, Instagram or travel channels.

02

Trust PLP

See real rooms, location and access information, plus official contact channels.

03

Ask about a stay

Send preferred dates, guest count and contact details using a simple enquiry form.

04

Confirm a reservation

Concierge checks availability, quotes approved rates and follows up.

We will use the existing PLP website and enterprise workspace as the foundation. A guest enquiry is never presented as an automatic confirmed booking.

03

What we will do, and when

A manageable sequence that starts with the booking process before expanding paid advertising.

Nov 23–24
First 48 hours

Get ready for enquiries

Test the current website on mobile. Prepare the stay-enquiry form, route enquiries to concierge, and verify the guest information needed to give accurate quotations.

Intended result: a clear path from visitor to concierge.
Nov 25–29
Launch week

Show the real property

Prepare three short authentic videos: arriving at PLP, getting to the beach, and room layout/capacity. Publish through approved social channels with one clear enquiry action.

Intended result: useful, trustworthy guest-facing content.
Nov 30–Dec 6
Week two

Build wider credibility

Prepare a genuine photo lookbook, evaluate suitable accommodation listings, and explore relevant travel advisors and creator partnerships.

Intended result: more ways for potential guests to discover and trust PLP.
December
Ongoing

Schedule and improve

Batch and schedule approved Facebook and Instagram content. Review real enquiries and bookings weekly, and consider advertising only once the enquiry process is working and the spending is approved.

Intended result: a repeatable marketing operation, not daily manual posting.
04

The first three videos

We do not need an expensive production campaign to start. Three real videos can answer the biggest questions and establish credibility.

VIDEO 01

From arrival to the view

Show the actual entrance, route into the accommodation and the view guests will experience.

VIDEO 02

Beach access and transport

Show how people actually get to White Beach and return. State travel times only after they are confirmed.

VIDEO 03

Rooms and sleeping arrangements

Show real beds, bathrooms and common areas. Confirm guest capacity before adding numbers.

Why this matters

When a new guest is deciding whether to book, practical evidence—real spaces, clear transport and prompt answers—can matter more than a beautifully edited advertisement.

05

Who we want to reach

We will focus on traveler groups with a clear reason to choose a private hillside accommodation, then refine the audience based on actual enquiries.

PRIMARY TEST

Families and groups

Filipino families, barkada trips, reunions and private getaways, matched to genuine room capacity.

SECONDARY TEST

Couples and occasions

Guests seeking a distinctive setting for holidays, milestones and quieter stays.

SECONDARY TEST

Balikbayans and visitors

Travelers who particularly value clear booking information, reliable communication and payment reassurance.

Where we reach them

Instagram and Facebook first, supported by the current PLP website; suitable accommodation platforms and travel partners follow when verified and commercially worthwhile.

06

Who will handle what

Doc does not need to manage daily posts or technical setup. The property team confirms guest-facing facts; the implementation team handles the marketing system.

Implementation team

  • Prepare the website's guest-enquiry journey.
  • Organize the social content calendar.
  • Prepare and schedule approved posts and videos.
  • Set up simple concierge follow-up tracking.
  • Summarize enquiries and confirmed bookings.

Doc and operating team

  • Confirm accommodation rates and capacities.
  • Provide accurate availability and reservation authority.
  • Confirm guest arrival and transport arrangements.
  • Approve official guest payment/deposit instructions.
  • Approve promotional spending separately if recommended.
07

Only four details needed to begin

A short conversation with Doc or the property manager should establish the immediate facts. Nothing should be advertised as included or available unless confirmed.

01 / STAY

Rates and capacities

What is the approved starting rate and how many people can stay in each unit?

02 / AVAILABILITY

Dates and confirmation

What can guests book in December–February, and who confirms a reservation?

03 / ACCESS

Arrival and transport

What transport arrangements can guests actually expect to and from the property?

04 / PAYMENT

Official deposit method

Which verified business payment or deposit instructions should concierge provide?

Doc's role is to approve commercial information, not to prepare technical documents. Routine permit-document collection is outside the immediate marketing workflow unless separately requested by a relevant institution.

08

How we measure results

We will report what leads to business, rather than judging success mainly by follower counts.

Qualified enquiries

People who provide genuine dates, guest numbers and accommodation needs.

Follow-up performance

How promptly and reliably the team replies, provides quotations and follows up.

Confirmed stays

Enquiries that become actual bookings—and the channel that brought them.

What is not part of this plan

It is not a public resort redesign, a new standalone booking system, a commitment to an advertising budget, or a Pearl Club membership launch. No forecasted revenue, rates, amenities or transport promises are being invented.

OWNER REVIEW · PROPOSED NEXT STEP

Approve the direction. Confirm the four facts. Start executing.

This plan is designed to use PLP's existing website and enterprise workspace to attract more suitable guests and turn genuine enquiries into confirmed stays. Any changes to rates, payment instructions or advertising spend require owner approval.

A clear, practical plan for Pueblo La Perla Boracay

Bringing more guests
to Pueblo La Perla.

We will make it easier for the right guests to discover PLP, feel confident about staying here, ask about availability, and complete their reservation with our team.

Presented to Dr. Marichi Fong Ramos   ·   Proposed launch: 23–29 November 2026
In one minute

What are we going to do?

We already have PLP's website and its online identity. Our next step is to turn them into a practical guest-enquiry system. We will show the property clearly, make booking questions easy to answer, and help the concierge follow up with every serious guest. The aim is not simply more followers. It is more qualified enquiries and more confirmed stays.

The guest's experience

Four simple steps from discovery to booking.

Every part of the marketing plan supports one of these steps.

01

Discover PLP

Guests see real property photos and short videos through Facebook, Instagram and suitable travel channels.

02

Feel confident

They understand the villa, where it is, how to get around, and how to contact the genuine PLP team.

03

Send an enquiry

They provide dates, guest count and contact details through one easy mobile form or concierge channel.

04

Confirm a stay

The concierge checks availability, gives accurate rates, follows up and confirms reservations through approved channels.

Social media attracts interest. The website and concierge turn that interest into real business. A submitted enquiry is not automatically a booking.

Keeping responsibilities simple

Our implementation work

What we will take care of

  • Improve the existing website enquiry journey.
  • Organize Facebook and Instagram content.
  • Prepare and schedule approved property posts and videos.
  • Build simple concierge follow-up tracking.
  • Report on real enquiries and confirmed bookings.
  • Evaluate travel listings and advertising only when ready.
Only four commercial confirmations

What we need from Doc or her team

  • Approved room or villa rates and how many guests each can accommodate.
  • Available dates and who has authority to confirm reservations.
  • Actual guest transport and beach-access arrangements.
  • Official payment and deposit instructions guests may safely use.

The relevant manager or reservations staff may supply the details. Doc only needs to confirm or approve the commercial decisions.

How we will know it is working

Not likes. Real guest interest and real stays.

We will start with a small, useful set of measures. There are no invented booking forecasts or promised revenue numbers.

01

Serious enquiries

How many people send genuine dates, guest counts and accommodation questions?

02

Timely follow-up

How quickly do we answer, quote, and follow up with prospective guests?

03

Confirmed stays

Which enquiries became real bookings, and where did those guests find PLP?

Any advertising budget will be proposed separately and requires approval. Pearl Club remains a separate initiative and will not be mixed into this stay-booking campaign.
